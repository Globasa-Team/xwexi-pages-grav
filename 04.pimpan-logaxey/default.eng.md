## Other Well Wishes

Note: As seen above, greetings with **bon** (good, well) are expressed as compounds, whereas farewells are expressed as two-word phrases. In the following expressions, compounds are used when the speaker and listener remain together. Hence, the two root words are together, combined into one word. In contrast, two-word phrases are used if either the speaker or the listener departs. Hence, the root words are apart.

[**bonata**](bonata.mp3) _or_ [**bon ata**](bon_ata.mp3) - welcome

[**bonxanse**](bonxanse.mp3) _or_ [**bon xanse**](bon_xanse.mp3) - good luck

[**bonyam**](bonyam.mp3) _or_ [**bon yam**](bon_yam.mp3) - bon appetit ("good meal")

[**bonglu**](bonglu.mp3) _or_ [**bon glu**](bon_glu.mp3) - cheers ("good drink")

[**bonturi**](bonturi.mp3) _or_ [**bon turi**](bon_turi.mp3) - bon voyage, safe travels

## Common Questions/Replies

* [**Yu sen kepul?**](Yu_sen_kepul.mp3) _or_ [**Yu kepul?**](Yu_kepul.mp3)

Very well, and you?

[**semibon**](semibon.mp3)

[**semi, semi**](semi_semi.mp3)  
so-so

* [**Yu name keto?**](Yu_name_keto.mp3) _or_ [**Yusu name sen keto?**](Yusu_name_sen_keto.mp3)  
What is your name?

[**Mi name...**](Mi_name.mp3) _or_ [**Misu name sen...**](Misu_name_sen.mp3)  
My name is...

[**(To sen) yukwe na xorkone yu.**](To_sen_yukwe_na_xorkone_yu.mp3) _or_ [**Yukwe.**](Yukwe.mp3)  
It's nice (pleasant) meeting you. or It's pleasant.

[**(To sen) furaha na xorkone yu.**](To_sen_furaha_na_xorkone_yu.mp3) _or_ [**Furaha.**](Furaha.mp3)  
It's a pleasure to meet you. or It's a pleasure.

[**(Mi sen) hox na xorkone yu.**](Mi_sen_hox_na_xorkone_yu.mp3) _or_ [**Mi sen hox.**](Mi_sen_hox.mp3)  
(I am) happy to meet you. or I am happy.

* [**Yu ogar keloka?**](Yu_ogar_keloka.mp3)  
Where do you live?

[**Mi ogar in...**](Mi_ogar_in.mp3)  
I live in...

* [**Yu sen of keloka?**](Yu_sen_of_keloka.mp3)  
Where are you from?

[**Mi sen of...**](Mi_sen_of.mp3)  
I'm from...

* [**Yu sen kemo lao?**](Yu_sen_kemo_lao.mp3) _or_ **Yu laoje kenumer nyan?**  
How old are you?

[**Mi sen lao fe... nyan.**](Mi_sen_lao_fe.mp3) _or_ [**Mi laoje... nyan.**](mi_laoje.mp3)  
I am... years old.

* [**Kam yu (pala) Globasa?**](Kam_yu_pala_globasa.mp3)  
Do you speak Globasa?

[**Si, xosu.**](Si_xosu.mp3) _or_ [**Si, mas no bon.**](si_mas_no_bon.mp3)  
Yes, a little (bit). or Yes, but not well.

* [**Yu pala ke basa?**](Yu_pala_ke_basa.mp3)  
What language(s) do you speak?

[**Mi pala...**](Mi_pala.mp3)  
I speak...

* [**Kam yu aham?**](Kam_yu_aham.mp3)  
Do you understand?

[**(Si,) mi aham.**](Si_mi_aham.mp3)  
(Yes,) I understand.

[**(No,) mi no aham.**](No_mi_no_aham.mp3)  
(No,) I don't understand.

* [**Ren loga... kemaner (in Globasa)?**](Ren_loga_kemaner.mp3)  
How do you say... (in Globasa)?

[**(In Globasa,) ren loga...**](In_globasa_ren_loga.mp3)  
(In Globasa,) you say...
